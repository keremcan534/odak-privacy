Privacy Policy

Who this policy is for

This policy covers the Android game Box Sort: Shelf Packing Puzzle by BlackBlue Studios (package com.keremcan534.cargopanic) and its web version. Inside the game, the title screen shows the game's name, Cargo Panic. On Google Play the same game is also listed under these local names: Kutu Yerleştirme: Raf Dizme (Turkish), Kisten sortieren: Regal-Puzzle (German), Ordenar cajas en estanterías (Spanish), Rangement de cartons : puzzle (French), Susun Kotak: Puzzle Rak Gudang (Indonesian), Ordina scatole sugli scaffali (Italian), Układanie pudełek na półkach (Polish), Jogo de Organizar Caixas (Portuguese) and Сортировка коробок на полках (Russian).

Summary

BlackBlue Studios does not collect, send or share any personal data through this game. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases, and it does not connect to the internet while you play.

What is stored, and where

Your progress (unlocked levels, stars, Endless records, the game in progress) and your settings (sound, vibration, 2D or 3D view, graphics quality, reduced motion, language) are saved only on your own device, in the app's local storage. They never leave the device and we cannot see them. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes this data. In the browser, clearing the site's data does the same.

Advertising

The game shows no ads. It contains no advertising SDK and does not read or use the device's advertising ID.

Purchases

There are no in-app purchases. The game does not use Google Play Billing and handles no payment information.

Permissions

The app asks for no special permissions. It declares vibration (for short haptic feedback, which you can turn off in the game's settings) and internet access, which the app framework requires; the game itself sends nothing over the network.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Third parties

No third-party services are built into the game. The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep standard server logs of visits under its own privacy statement.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that handles personal data (for example advertising), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.

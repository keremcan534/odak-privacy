Privacy Policy

Summary

This app does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. It has no account, no advertising, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no tracking. It does not have the internet permission, so it cannot connect to the internet. The rival snakes in the game are computer-controlled bots running on your device; there are no online players.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (coins, the skins you unlocked and use, your best lengths, wins, snakes eaten and games played) and your settings (language, controls, sound, vibration) are stored only in the app's storage on your device. We cannot see or access any of this. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes everything.

If Android's own backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app's data in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is managed by Android and your Google account; we have no access to it.

Ratings

After you play a number of matches the app may ask Google Play to show its own rating card. Whether it appears, and what you enter there, is handled by Google Play; the app receives nothing from it.

Permissions

  • Vibration: for light haptic feedback, for example when you crash or eat another snake. You can turn it off in the app's settings.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Privacy Policy

Summary

We (BlackBlue Studios) do not collect, receive or store any personal data. The app has no account, no in-app purchases and no analytics of its own. The game works offline and your progress stays on your device. From version 1.1.0 the app shows ads provided by Google AdMob, a third-party service that collects some data as described below.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Now and then, when you leave the result of a finished game with Play again or Home, a full-screen ad is shown. There are never ads during a game or in your first games of a session. These ads are served by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited). To show, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • device and advertising identifiers (the Android advertising ID, an app set ID);
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • diagnostic and app-interaction information about the ads (for example ad views and taps, crash and performance logs).

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland) the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are requested, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's Google settings. Google's use of this data is described in its policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We do not receive this data.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (your wins, losses and draws against the computer, the number of two-player games, the puzzle you reached, the boards and stones you unlocked and any unfinished game) and your settings (language, sound, vibration, fast stones, highlighted pits, chosen board and stones, the last game options you picked) are stored only in the app's storage on your device. We cannot see or access any of this. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes everything.

If Android's own backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app's data in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is managed by Android and your Google account; we have no access to it.

Ratings

After you win a few games the app may ask Google Play to show its own rating card. Whether it appears, and what you enter there, is handled by Google Play; the app receives nothing from it.

Permissions

  • Vibration: for light haptic feedback, for example when you pick a pit or capture stones. You can turn it off in the app's settings.
  • Internet and network state: used only by the ad SDK to load ads.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ad SDK for ads (see above).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience with parental guidance.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Reklamlar (Google AdMob)

Reklam kimliğini cihazının Google ayarlarından sıfırlayabilir, silebilir veya kişiselleştirilmiş reklamları kapatabilirsin.

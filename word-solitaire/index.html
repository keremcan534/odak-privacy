Privacy Policy

Summary

This app does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. It has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (the level you are on and the game in progress) and your settings are stored only in the app's storage on your device so you can continue where you left off. We cannot see or access this data. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes everything.

If Android's own backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app's data in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is managed by Android and your Google account; we have no access to it.

Permissions

  • Vibration: for the optional haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.
  • Network access (INTERNET): declared by the app framework. The app itself makes no network requests.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

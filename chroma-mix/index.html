Privacy Policy

Summary

Chroma Mix is a color puzzle game: you mix color bubbles to fill the orders on screen. We (BlackBlue Studios) do not collect, receive or store any personal data. The app has no account, no analytics of its own and does not use the advertising ID. The game works offline and your progress stays on your device. The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob that are suitable for all ages and not personalized; AdMob is a third-party service that processes some data as described below.

Data stored on your device

Your high score, your total score, your hints and your settings (background, sound and display options) are stored only in the app's storage on your device. We cannot see or access any of this. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes everything.

If Android's own backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app's data in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is managed by Android and your Google account; we have no access to it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited). Every ad request is marked as child-directed, so the ads are:

  • limited to content suitable for all ages (rated G);
  • not personalized: no interest-based ads and no remarketing, and nothing you do in the app is used to build an advertising profile.

The app does not use the Android advertising ID and does not ask for the advertising ID permission. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may still process your IP address (for example to find your approximate country), device and app information (for example device model, Android version, language and app version), information about the ads shown and tapped, and diagnostic information. Google processes this data under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We do not receive this data.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: used only by the ad SDK to load ads. The game itself works offline.

Children

Chroma Mix is suitable for all ages, including children. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the app may be used by children, every ad request is tagged for child-directed treatment under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and the app follows the Google Play Families policy: ads come only from Google AdMob, are limited to content suitable for all ages, are never personalized and are not based on the advertising ID. Parents with questions can contact us at the address below.

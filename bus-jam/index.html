Privacy Policy

Summary

We (BlackBlue Studios) do not collect, receive or store any personal data. The app has no account and no analytics of its own. The game works offline and your progress stays on your device. The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob; AdMob is a third-party service that collects some data as described below.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (which levels you finished) and your settings are stored only in the app's storage on your device. We cannot see or access any of this. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes everything.

If Android's own backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app's data in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is managed by Android and your Google account; we have no access to it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows a full-screen ad now and then between two levels, never during a level, and at most once every few minutes. These ads are served by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited). To show, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • device and advertising identifiers (the Android advertising ID, an app set ID);
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • device and app information (for example device model, Android version, language and app version);
  • information about your interactions with ads (for example ad views and taps);
  • diagnostic information (for example crash and performance logs).

Google processes this data under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We do not receive this data.

Your choices

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland) the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are requested, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices (this row appears where the consent form applies).

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's settings: open Settings → Google → All services → Ads (on some devices Settings → Privacy → Ads) and choose Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID. Once you delete it, apps and ad SDKs no longer receive an advertising ID from your device.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: used only by the ad SDK to load ads.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ad SDK for ads (see above).
  • Vibration: for light haptic feedback while playing. You can turn it off in the app's settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Ads are limited to content rated for a general audience with parental guidance.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Reklamlar (Google AdMob)

Uygulama iki bölüm arasında ara sıra tam ekran bir reklam gösterir; bir bölüm sırasında asla göstermez ve birkaç dakikada en fazla bir kez gösterir.   • cihaz ve uygulama bilgileri (örneğin cihaz modeli, Android sürümü, dil ve uygulama sürümü);

Seçimlerin

Yasanın gerektirdiği yerlerde (örneğin Avrupa Ekonomik Alanı, Birleşik Krallık ve İsviçre) uygulama, reklam istenmeden önce Google'ın onay formuyla iznini sorar; seçimini istediğin zaman Ayarlar → Reklam gizlilik seçenekleri'nden değiştirebilirsin (bu satır, onay formunun geçerli olduğu yerlerde görünür).

Reklam kimliğini cihazının ayarlarından sıfırlayabilir, silebilir veya kişiselleştirilmiş reklamları kapatabilirsin: Ayarlar → Google → Tüm hizmetler → Reklamlar'ı (bazı cihazlarda Ayarlar → Gizlilik → Reklamlar) açıp Reklam kimliğini sıfırla veya Reklam kimliğini sil'i seç. Sildikten sonra uygulamalar ve reklam SDK'ları cihazından reklam kimliği alamaz.

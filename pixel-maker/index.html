Privacy Policy

Summary

This app does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. It has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not have the internet permission, so it cannot connect to the internet.

Data stored on your device

Your drawings and your settings are stored only in the app's storage on your device. We cannot see or access any of this. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes everything, so export drawings you want to keep.

Photos you choose

When you turn a photo into pixel art or add one to trace over, you pick it yourself in the system file picker. The app reads only that picture, converts it on your device and never uploads it. It has no permission to browse your photos on its own.

Sharing

When you export, the picture is handed to the Android share sheet and goes only where you send it.

If Android's own backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app's data in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is managed by Android and your Google account; we have no access to it.

Permissions

  • Vibration: for light haptic feedback on buttons. You can turn it off in the app's settings.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

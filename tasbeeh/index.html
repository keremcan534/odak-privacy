Privacy Policy

Summary

This app does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. It has no account, no advertising, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no tracking. It does not have the internet permission, so it cannot connect to the internet.

Data stored on your device

Your counts and settings are stored only in the app's storage on your device: the counter you use and where each counter stands, the counts you set, the dhikr you add yourself, the total of each dhikr, how much you counted on each day (for the daily goal, the days in a row and the charts), your daily goal, the reminder time, your language, theme, bead style, vibration, sound, volume key and screen settings, and how many times you used the app (for the rating card below). We cannot see or access any of this. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes everything; the statistics can also be cleared in the app.

If Android's own backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app's data in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is managed by Android and your Google account; we have no access to it.

Daily reminder

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks Android to show a notification at the time you choose. Android asks you first whether the app may show notifications. The reminder is scheduled on your device; nothing is sent anywhere.

Ratings

After you have used the app a few times it may ask Google Play to show its own rating card. Whether it appears, and what you enter there, is handled by Google Play; the app receives nothing from it.

Permissions

  • Vibration: a short vibration for each count and a longer one at the end of a round, if you leave vibration on.
  • Notifications, run at startup and prevent phone from sleeping: used only for the daily reminder, so it can be shown at its time and kept after the phone restarts.

When you turn on counting with the volume keys, the app reads only presses of the volume keys, and only while its counter is open on the screen.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
